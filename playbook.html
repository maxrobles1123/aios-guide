The AIOS Playbook

AIOS gives you four AI agents that work for you inside Claude. This playbook takes you through each one: what it does for you, and exactly what to type to put it to work.

How this playbook works

Each agent gets its own step: what it does, the words and phrases it uses, and what to type to get each job done.

You don’t have to choose an agent

Type what you need, and AIOS will hand it to the correct agent to get the job done.

How each request is laid out

Every request in this playbook uses the same four labels:

When
The moment the request is for.
Type
The exact words to copy into AIOS. Replace anything in [brackets] with your own details.
You’ll get
What AIOS sends back.
Check
What to read before anything goes out.

AIOS helps you decide. It doesn’t decide for you.

It won’t make decisions, or act on them, on your behalf. Its job is to make sure nothing falls through the cracks, so you have what you need to talk to the right people at the right time. Anything for a client stays a draft until you send it or type yes, nothing gets published, and nothing on your calendar changes unless you say so.

Chief of Staff

Your Chief of Staff makes sure your days and weeks line up with your priorities and the promises you've made. It reads your email and calendar, then tells you what needs your attention before you have to go looking for it.

What it does when you ask

  • Sends you three emails on a schedule: a Morning Brief, an End of Day summary and a Sunday Planner.
  • Lists what needs your attention first, and writes drafts for you to review and send.
  • Plans your week around the goals in your Success Blueprint.
  • When someone is late on something they promised you, it drafts a polite reminder for you to send (no more than one every three days).

What it does without being asked

  • Morning BriefEvery weekday at 7:00 AMWhat needs your attention first, what you owe and are waiting on, today's calendar, and drafts ready for you to send.
  • End of DayEvery weekday at 5:00 PMWhat got done, what is still open, and what to start on tomorrow.
  • Sunday PlannerEvery Sunday at 5:00 PMYour week ahead, built around the goals in your Success Blueprint.

These arrive in your email while your computer is on and Claude is open.

Means: Your week ahead, built around the goals in your Success Blueprint.Where you’ll see it: In your email, every Sunday at 5:00 PM.

Prospecting Agent

Your Prospecting Agent helps you turn new inquiries, and the people who already know you, into real conversations.

What it does when you ask

  • Writes a reply to each new inquiry that answers the person's question first.
  • Each morning, picks up to five people worth a personal note, each with a real reason to reach out.
  • Finds buyers and sellers you did real work for whose plans went quiet.
  • Keeps your contacts sorted, and saves the calls, texts and coffees you tell it about.

What it does without being asked

Each morning, your Morning Brief lists new inquiries, with anyone who wants something today at the top. It also prepares Today's five under Ready for you.

A phrase you’ll see from your Prospecting Agent, and what it means

Today's five
Means: Up to five people worth a personal note today, each with a real reason to reach out.Where you’ll see it: In your Morning Brief, under Ready for you.

Client Experience Agent

Your Client Experience Agent prepares you for every meeting, records what happened afterward, and keeps track of every promise: the ones you made and the ones made to you.

What it does when you ask

  • Gives you a short briefing before a listing appointment, a showing or any meeting.
  • Turns a recording or a quick summary of a meeting into notes, a recap email draft and a note for your CRM.
  • Keeps your Promise Tracker: what you owe people, and what you're waiting on.
  • Opens new seller deals, records closings, and schedules a check-in after the move.

What it does without being asked

It picks up promises from your email and meetings, and marks one done when it sees the sent email that delivers it (a draft doesn't count). Your Promise Tracker sheet in your AIOS folder is rebuilt every morning; to change a promise, tell AIOS.

How it sets due dates when you say…

“By Friday,” “end of week” or “EOD”
Due at 5:00 PM that day.
“Soon,” “ASAP” or “next week”
No date is set. AIOS asks you for one.
No timing at all
Due in about three days, marked (assumed). AIOS confirms the date with you and never sends a reminder based on it.

Words and phrases you’ll see from your Client Experience Agent, and what they mean

Promise Tracker
Means: Every promise you've made, and every promise made to you, in one place.Where you’ll see it: In your Morning Brief, in meeting briefings, and as a sheet in your AIOS folder.
(assumed)
Means: A due date AIOS set because none was given. It confirms the date with you before relying on it.Where you’ll see it: Next to a date in your Promise Tracker.

Marketing Agent

Your Marketing Agent writes the marketing for your listings in your voice, using the facts you confirm and CSC's approved marketing prompts.

What it does when you ask

  • Builds a complete three-phase campaign for a listing from one request.
  • Writes listing descriptions, social posts, video scripts and just listed emails.
  • Finds the ZIP codes your buyer is most likely to come from.
  • Saves everything in the deal's folder.

What it does without being asked

Nothing. It works only when you ask, and it never publishes anything; you post and send everything yourself.

Words and phrases you’ll see from your Marketing Agent, and what they mean

Held: fair-housing review needed
Means: A marketing piece with words that need changing before you use it. AIOS names the words and says why.Where you’ll see it: At the top of the piece.
Experimental
Means: A ZIP code AIOS estimated, rather than one the market research showed.Where you’ll see it: Next to a ZIP code in a feeder market report.

What to type, and when

Each of the 6 requests below starts with a thought you might have. Under it is what to type when you have it.

Anything you skipped stays in [brackets] for you to fill in.

Every fact, price and date, plus your brokerage and license line.

If something goes wrong

Find what you’re seeing below, then open it for the fix.

Your briefs

A brief says it couldn't check my email or calendar

Reconnect the account it names, then ask for the brief again. When a box appears, click Connect and sign in.

TypeReconnect my Gmail.
Reconnect my calendar.
Reconnect my Outlook.
Refresh my brief.
My brief is late, or didn't come

Briefs only run while your computer is on and Claude is open. Ask AIOS anything in your AIOS Project: it notices a brief that didn't run and runs it on the spot. You can also ask for it directly:

TypeRun my morning brief.
I want my briefs at a different time

Tell AIOS in your own words, for example “Start my brief at 6:30” or “No weekend briefs.”

Outlook asks for an administrator when I reconnect

That's a setting at your company. Ask your IT admin to allow the Microsoft 365 connector for Claude.

Your AIOS folder and chats

AIOS says it can't see my AIOS folder

Click Allow for this session (or Allow). If that doesn't work, start a new chat inside your AIOS Project and ask again.

My AIOS chats are scattered

Start each AIOS chat from your AIOS Project, so they stay together under AIOS.
