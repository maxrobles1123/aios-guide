    Install went wrong

    I do not have Claude yet.
    1. Go to claude.ai/download.
    2. Click the download for Windows or Mac. Open it.
    3. Open the Claude app. Sign in.
    4. Choose a plan, Pro or higher. You pay Anthropic for it.
    I cannot find Customize or Connectors.

    Look down the left side of Claude for Customize. Click it, then click Connectors at the top. Still not there? Update the Claude app and try again.

    The code email did not come.

    Wait 2 minutes. Check Spam or Junk for “Your AIOS sign-in code”. In Gmail, also check Promotions and Updates.

    Still nothing? On the sign-in page, click Send a new code.

    The code does not work.

    Use the code from the newest email. Type all 6 digits. Codes last 10 minutes.

    More install questions
    Copy and paste did not work.

    Click Copy again. Click once in the box in Claude. Press Ctrl + V (Mac: Cmd + V). Or type it.

    It says it could not find AIOS access for my email.

    Use the email you bought with. Your receipt or welcome email shows which one. Still stuck after two tries? Email us.

    It says “Too many tries”.

    Stop. Wait the time it shows. Then try once.

    I do not see Schedule or Trusted folders.

    You may be on the Claude website. Open the Claude app on your computer.

    AIOS says it cannot find my folder.
    1. In the Claude app, open Settings.
    2. Click Desktop app, then Trusted folders.
    3. Add your AIOS folder.
    4. Go back to your chat. Type continue.
    I stopped halfway.

    Open your AIOS Project. Type finish my AIOS setup. AIOS keeps what you already did.

    Still need help? Email info@chiragshahcoaching.com. Tell us what you see on your screen.

    AIOS is set up, but something is off

    My brief did not come.

    Check that Claude is open, your computer is awake and the scheduled task is active. You can also ask AIOS to run your Morning Brief now.

    Claude says AIOS is disconnected.
    1. In Claude, click Customize on the left, then Connectors.
    2. Click AIOS. A page says “You're not connected to AIOS yet.” Click Connect.
    3. Sign in with your email and a new code.

    Your records are still in the AIOS folder on your computer.

    How do I learn what each AI agent can do?

    Open The Playbook. It shows what each agent does, and the exact words to type.

    My brief says my email or calendar could not be checked.

    Type reconnect my email or reconnect my calendar. Click Connect and sign in.

    More questions
    My AIOS chats are all over the place.

    Start your AIOS chats from your AIOS Project. Click Projects, then AIOS. Type in its message box.

    Or, on the Claude home screen, pick AIOS in the Project picker under the message box.

    Chats you start there are listed under AIOS on the left.

    Can I turn it off?

    Yes. Open Schedule in the Claude app. Set each AIOS brief to Paused.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Setup needs a computer. After that, your briefs come by email, so you can read them on your phone.

    What if I cancel Claude?

    AIOS needs an eligible Claude account to operate inside Claude. Your local AIOS files remain on your computer.

    A question before I start

    Will it email my clients?

    AIOS prepares client emails for your review. It does not send them without your approval. Scheduled briefs are sent only to your own email when enabled.

    Who can see my client information?

    AIOS keeps its working client and deal records in a folder on your computer. Information you share through Claude and connected services is also processed by those providers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

    Does it replace me?

    No. AIOS helps prepare and organize the work. You're still the person advising clients, building relationships, negotiating and making decisions.

    Do I need to be technical?

    You don't need technical experience. Setup does require a computer and a few steps. The Install AIOS page walks you through them.

    More questions
    I use Outlook.

    Gmail and Google Calendar are currently supported. Outlook isn't available for member setup yet.

    What is Claude?

    An app made by a company called Anthropic. Software, not a person. AIOS works inside it.

    Do I pay for Claude?

    Yes. You need your own Claude plan, Pro or higher. You pay Anthropic for it, not Chirag Shah Coaching.

    Does it replace my CRM?

    No. Keep using your CRM as you do now.

    Can an AI answer my Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp messages?

    That's Meta's own Business Agent, a separate tool from AIOS. If you switch it on, set it up in this order:

    1. Write what it may answer: your services, your areas, your hours, how to book you.
    2. Write what it must not answer, and check the two lists don't contradict each other.
    3. Name who it hands people over to, and what it says right before it does.
    4. Keep it telling people it's an AI.
    5. Never let it qualify, screen or sort buyers or renters. That's a fair housing line.

    Try it with five real-sounding questions before you leave it on.
